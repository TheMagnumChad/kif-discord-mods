HOENN NATIVE OVERWORLD
For Kuray’s Infinite Fusion 6.8.2
Version 0.12.24 RC1
Developed by TheMagnumChad

Brings the native Hoenn-style roaming Pokémon system to the overworld. Wild Pokémon appear on routes, in caves, towns, and suitable waters; they roam, react to each other, and can occasionally fuse spontaneously. Includes shiny overworld effects, sneak-up surprise battles, and configurable spawn settings.
